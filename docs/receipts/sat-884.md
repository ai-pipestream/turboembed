# SaT on devices against the CPU (#884)

`analysis/sat/tests/device.rs` runs the SaT step on the CPU and on the
device `TURBO_TEST_DEVICE` names, with the same bundle, and holds every
token's boundary probability to the tier's bound of the CPU's (1e-4 at
f32, 1e-2 at f16); tokens the threshold puts on the other side must have
a CPU probability within that bound of it, and are listed. The packed
test reads 36 documents together, so that runs fill, at the bundle's
max_batch and at 3 rows a run, against each document read alone on the
CPU. `tests/speed.rs` alternates a CPU pass with a device pass and gives
the median of each.

Bundles: `testdata/tiny-sat-bundle` (2 layers, width 32) and
`sat-1l-sm` at `00a783439c201b20ee91ee1624dc817f1bf0e578` sealed from
`bundle/recipes/sat-1l-sm.json` with xlm-roberta-base's tokenizer
(#1020; manifest `c3a0dbcc`), named by `TURBO_TEST_SAT_BUNDLE`.

## CUDA: RTX 4080 SUPER

Driver 615.71.09, nvcc 12.4.131, release build, `--features turbo/cuda`.

```
TURBO_TEST_DEVICE=cuda TURBO_TEST_PRECISION=<p> [TURBO_TEST_SAT_BUNDLE=<sat-1l-sm>] \
  cargo test --release --locked -p turbo-sat --features turbo/cuda --test device --test parity -- --nocapture
```

`tests/device.rs`: 3 of 3 pass at `model`, `fastest` and `exact` on
both bundles. Largest probability difference against the CPU:

| Bundle | Tier | (0.5, 16, 62) | (0.5, 7, 30) | (0.25, 62, 62) | Packed, max_batch | Packed, 3 rows | Near the threshold |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| tiny | `model`, `exact` (f32) | 6.0e-7 | 5.4e-7 | 6.3e-7 | 5.4e-7 | 5.4e-7 | none |
| tiny | `fastest` (f16) | 1.9e-3 | 1.8e-3 | 2.4e-3 | 2.4e-3 | 2.4e-3 | 4 in each packed run, CPU 0.24932 against 0.25 |
| sat-1l-sm | `model`, `exact` (f32) | 8.9e-7 | 8.9e-7 | 8.9e-7 | 8.9e-7 | 1.2e-6 | none |
| sat-1l-sm | `fastest` (f16) | 2.5e-3 | 1.9e-3 | 2.5e-3 | 4.6e-3 | 3.3e-3 | none |

The packed runs read 284 windows (tiny) and 208 (sat-1l-sm). The four
tiny `fastest` tokens are one token of a text repeated in four
documents, 6.8e-4 under the threshold on the CPU.

`tests/parity.rs` against wtpsplit: at `model` and `exact` all six
pass, with the same Seen figures as the CPU, and the released
checkpoint passes too. A model of limited lookahead is refused on the
device (`turbo::model::runs_band`), which the lookahead test now checks
there. At `fastest` the four tiny-fixture tests and the released one
fail over the 1e-3 allowed against wtpsplit (largest 1.1e-3), as
docs/neural-annotators.md records.

Speed, `TURBO_SPEED_RUNS=7`, CPU at `model` beside the device at the
tier named. The GPU was shared with desktop applications (about 26%
busy, no compute jobs), so the ratios carry more weight than the
times; SM clock 2610 MHz throughout.

| Bundle | Tokens | Tier | CPU ms a pass | CUDA ms a pass | CUDA tokens/s | Ratio |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| tiny (block 62, stride 62) | 16464 | `model` | 8.0 | 3.9 | 4,200,000 | 2.1x |
| tiny | 16464 | `fastest` | 6.9 | 3.3 | 4,940,000 | 2.1x |
| sat-1l-sm (block 510, stride 64) | 11612 | `model` | 445.3 | 55.3 | 210,000 | 8.1x |
| sat-1l-sm | 11612 | `fastest` | 480.5 | 29.2 | 398,000 | 16.5x |

Sentences found: the same on both sides for sat-1l-sm (676); 9224 on
CUDA at `fastest` against 9220 on the CPU for the tiny bundle, from the
near-threshold tokens above.

## Level Zero: Arc Pro B70

compute-runtime 26.27.39122.14 (libze-intel-gpu1, intel-opencl-icd),
Level Zero loader 1.32.0, IGC 2.40.14, oneAPI 2025.3.3, in-tree xe
driver; release build, `--features turbo/levelzero` and, separately,
`turbo/levelzero-onednn`. No `sat-1l-sm` bundle was on the machine, so
these rows are the tiny bundle only. No other client used the compute
engine (xe fdinfo); a desktop client held the render engine about 68%
busy throughout.

```
TURBO_TEST_DEVICE=levelzero TURBO_TEST_PRECISION=<p> \
  cargo test --release --locked -p turbo-sat --features turbo/levelzero --test device --test parity -- --nocapture
```

`tests/device.rs`: 3 of 3 pass at `model`, `fastest` and `exact` in both
builds. Largest probability difference against the CPU:

| Build | Tier | (0.5, 16, 62) | (0.5, 7, 30) | (0.25, 62, 62) | Packed, max_batch | Packed, 3 rows | Near the threshold |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| levelzero | `model`, `exact` (f32) | 8.9e-7 | 6.0e-7 | 8.9e-7 | 6.9e-7 | 5.1e-7 | none |
| levelzero | `fastest` (f16) | 1.6e-3 | 1.5e-3 | 1.7e-3 | 1.9e-3 | 1.9e-3 | none |
| levelzero-onednn | `model` (f32) | 8.9e-7 | 6.0e-7 | 8.9e-7 | 6.0e-7 | 5.1e-7 | none |
| levelzero-onednn | `fastest` (f16) | 5.0e-3 | 5.0e-3 | 5.0e-3 | 2.7e-3 | 2.7e-3 | 1 to 8 a setting, each CPU probability within 1.1e-3 of the threshold |

`tests/parity.rs` against wtpsplit: 6 of 6 pass at `model` and `exact`
in both builds, with the CPU's Seen figures to within 1e-7; the
lookahead model is refused on the device. At `fastest` the same four
tiny-fixture tests fail as on CUDA, over the 1e-3 allowed against
wtpsplit: largest 1.3e-3 with the own kernels, 1.6e-3 with oneDNN.

Speed, `TURBO_SPEED_RUNS=7`, tiny bundle (16464 tokens, block 62,
stride 62), CPU (a Ryzen 9 9950X) at `model` beside the device:

| Tier | CPU ms a pass | Level Zero ms a pass (fastest to slowest) | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `model` | 6.4 | 29.8 (8.2 to 41.1) | 0.21x |
| `fastest` | 6.4 | 29.3 (7.4 to 40.7) | 0.22x |

On a model of two layers of width 32 the device's per-run overhead
dominates, and the shared render engine widened its spread; a
real-size bundle has not been timed on the B70.
