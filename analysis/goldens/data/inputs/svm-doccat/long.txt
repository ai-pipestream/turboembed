Weekend report

The weather turned on Saturday morning. Fog lay in the valley until noon, the wind picked up from the west, and by the evening heavy rain and hail had reached the coast. Snow fell on the hills overnight and the roads froze.

In the kitchen the plan held: we baked bread in a hot oven, simmered a soup of onions, garlic and fresh herbs, and served it with butter and cheese.

The cup final went ahead in the rain. The team scored a late goal, the keeper made two saves, and the fans in the stadium stayed until the referee blew for the end of the match.

On Monday the market opened lower. Bank shares fell as investors worried about rates and profits, while gold rose and bond yields climbed.
