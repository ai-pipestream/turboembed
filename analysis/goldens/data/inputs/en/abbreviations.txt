Dr. Alvarez moved to the U.S. in 1998 and opened a clinic on St. Mark's Place. She worked there with Mr. Chen until Jan. 2004, when the clinic closed. Prof. Okafor, Ph.D., later wrote that the U.S. health system "was not ready for her ideas." It is now run by a nonprofit, i.e. a charity, and sees about 40 patients a day.
